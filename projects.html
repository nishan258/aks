Projects

Selected current research themes and projects identified through UBC Forestry include:

High-Integrity and Inclusive Forestry Offset Carbon Markets

Research on the design, integrity and inclusiveness of forest carbon markets and forestry offset systems.

Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Through Bamboo

Work examining the role of bamboo forests and bamboo products in climate adaptation, mitigation and carbon management.

Conservation Under Global Change

Research on threatened species and ecosystems in Asia and South Africa under changing climatic and socio-ecological conditions.

Inclusive and Resilient Area-Based Conservation

Developing new approaches to protected areas that combine ecological integrity, resilience and social inclusion.

Community-Based Forest Restoration

Evaluation of the socio-ecological outcomes of community-based forest restoration in Asia.

Innovation in Online Learning

Evaluating learning activities and open education approaches that advance active, inclusive and collaborative learning in online forestry education.
