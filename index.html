UBC Profile

Faculty of Forestry & Environmental Stewardship

Conservation, climate change and sustainable forest management

Dr. Anil Shrestha is a Lecturer and Senior Researcher in UBC’s Faculty of Forestry & Environmental Stewardship. His work addresses biodiversity loss, deforestation and climate change through interdisciplinary research on threatened species and ecosystems, forest carbon markets, protected areas, restoration and forestry education.
