Teaching

My teaching focuses on forestry, conservation science and sustainable forest management, with an emphasis on active, inclusive and learner-centred approaches that build research and critical-thinking skills.

Teaching Areas

  • Forest wildlife ecology and management
  • Conservation science
  • Sustainable forest management
  • Online and open forestry education

Teaching and Learning Scholarship

I am involved in developing, implementing and evaluating innovative open educational resources for sustainable forest management in the Asia-Pacific. This work emphasizes collaborative learning, active participation and the application of research to complex conservation problems.

UBC records also identify Dr. Shrestha as an instructor for FRST 395: Forest Wildlife Ecology & Management.
