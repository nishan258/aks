Research

My research focuses on practical and interdisciplinary responses to global environmental change, with particular attention to conservation, forestry and the relationships between ecosystems and people.

Biodiversity and Threatened Species

Conservation of threatened species and ecosystems under global change, including the effects of climate change and human disturbance.

Forest Carbon Markets

High-integrity and inclusive forest carbon markets, forestry offsets, carbon management and the role of forests in climate mitigation.

Protected Areas

Developing inclusive, sustainable and resilient approaches to protected areas and area-based conservation.

Climate Change Adaptation

Adaptation and mitigation strategies for species, ecosystems and forest landscapes, including work on bamboo and bamboo products.

Forest Restoration and Livelihoods

Socio-ecological outcomes of community-based forest restoration and land-use change, with attention to livelihoods and equity.

Forestry Education

Active, inclusive and learner-centred teaching, online forestry education and open educational resources for sustainable forest management.
