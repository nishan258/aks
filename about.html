About

Dr. Anil Shrestha is a Lecturer and Senior Researcher in the Faculty of Forestry & Environmental Stewardship at the University of British Columbia. He is affiliated with the Department of Forest Resources Management and the Asia Forest Research Centre.

He completed his PhD in Wildlife Ecology at Wageningen University & Research in the Netherlands. His interdisciplinary conservation experience spans Nepal, Norway, South Africa, China and Canada.

His research and teaching focus on solution-oriented global environmental challenges, particularly biodiversity loss, deforestation and climate change. His work combines conservation science, socio-ecological analysis, evidence synthesis and applied approaches to forest and protected-area management.

Dr. Shrestha also contributes to forestry education, including the development and evaluation of innovative open educational resources and learner-centred approaches for sustainable forest management education in the Asia-Pacific region.
